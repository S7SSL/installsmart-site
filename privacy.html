Enquiries that do not become work are kept for up to 12 months. Client records are kept for six years after the work ends, for tax and legal reasons.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, or ask for a copy. Email sat@installsmart.ai. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

We only set Google Analytics cookies if you click Accept on the cookie banner. If you reject, Analytics runs without cookies and without identifying you. You can change your choice at any time with the Cookie settings link in the footer.
